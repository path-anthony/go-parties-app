01

Essence

Matter-of-fact with a grin. Chill, confident, never salesy, never cute. The product's one job: make throwing a party feel like two minutes of easy decisions, whether it's a kids' birthday, a backyard cigar night, a wedding, or a company field day. Same ease on every route.

02

Naming

In the app

GO! EVENT GROUP as the wordmark, orange GO!, charcoal EVENT GROUP. The app has no name of its own. It's GO's app.

On listings and paper

The Go Event Group. One name, one address, one phone, everywhere.

The assistant

Ask GO. A knowledgeable crew member, not a character or a mascot.

Banned

"Party in a box." Any invented product name. Any sub-brand.

03

Color

Cream ground, white cards, charcoal type, taupe labels. Orange is the single accent and it appears once per screen, on the action. If two things are orange, one is wrong. No dark screens; dark lives only inside photo gradients and the dark button. The app is locked to light mode so a phone on dark mode cannot flip it.

05

Imagery

Real photos by Mel. Candid, warm, daylight, real Connecticut backyards and venues, the real crew. No stock, no renders. Every photo gets a bottom gradient to charcoal so white type can sit on it.

Until the photos exist, the app uses photo plates: warm gradients with a faint texture, corner marks, and a spec line naming the shot Mel owes and its ratio. The spec lines are her shot list. Swapping a plate for a photo changes one file and nothing else moves.

07

Motion

Elements rise 10px and fade in over 380ms. Reveals open over 300ms. No page wipes, no parallax, no bouncing. Motion confirms, it never decorates.

08

Copy voice

Say the thing. Short sentences, verbs first. Matter-of-fact, then a little warmth. Prices are always visible and always real. Talk about the party, not the company. Assume the reader is competent.

Punctuation

No exclamation points; the grin is in the rhythm. No em dashes; use a period, a comma, or "and". Sentence case everywhere except labels.

Numbers

Numerals always: 2 hours, 25 people, $1,899. Times as 10 AM. Dates as Sat Sep 13. "from $1,600" when it varies.

SOUNDS LIKE US

Here's your party.

Ballpark? No wrong answer.

DOESN'T

Create unforgettable memories with our premier event experiences!

We're so excited to help you plan your dream celebration.

Elevate your special day with a seamless, one of a kind package.

Please select your preferred options below.

Banned words: unforgettable, elevate, seamless, one of a kind, dream, magical, premier. The full string bank lives in docs/BRAND.md section 10 and grows as screens get built.

09

Ask GO

A knowledgeable crew member, not a chatbot. It sounds exactly like the copy. It never opens itself: three doors only, the dashed line on Home, "Ask about this package", and the Ask tab. Every answer is one to three sentences and ends with an action, then it hands control back. It only recommends real packages and real add-ons at real prices, never invents inventory, never promises availability it hasn't checked, and hands custom quotes, large weddings, and big corporate jobs to Mel or Andy.

Backyard bday, 25 kids, about $1,500
Kids Birthday Deluxe at $1,599. Bounce house, a magician hour, face painting, cotton candy and snow cones. Right at the number.
Build that
Can you do 200 people for a gala in October?
That's a custom build. I'd have Andy scope it with you. Want me to send it over with your date?
Send to Andy
  • Price language: exact when exact, "from" when it varies, "about" only for add-on totals.
  • One better option, offered once. No pressure, no countdowns, no scarcity theater.
  • Uncertainty said plainly. Never "Great question". Never an apology for being an AI.
  • No emoji, no exclamation points, same as everywhere else.
10

Platform

  • Mobile first at 390px. On desktop the app centers at 560px with more air. There is no separate desktop layout.
  • Browsers: Safari iOS, Chrome Android, Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge. Internet Explorer retired in 2022 and is not supported.
  • Touch targets 44px minimum. Contrast 4.5:1 minimum; muted text only at 12px and up.
  • Fixed nav uses the iOS safe-area inset so it never collides with the home indicator.
  • Stack: Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind v4, shadcn/ui, Lucide, React Router. Static data. Vercel previews per push.
